. :
  • visible (Default): The content is not clipped and renders outside the element's box.
  • hidden: The content is clipped, and any content extending past the bounds of the box becomes invisible. No scrollbars are provided.
  • scroll: The content is clipped, but the browser always displays scrollbars (both horizontal and vertical), whether the content overflows or not.
  • auto: The smartest value—scrollbars are hidden by default and only appear if the content actually overflows the box.
