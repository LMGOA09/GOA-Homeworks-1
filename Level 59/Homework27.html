1. My Favorite Movie

About the Movie

Hot Dog Is a Qartvelian crime-drama/noir serial directed by Zaza Urushadze and Otar Shamatava, spanning 5 seasons; features protagonists Bidzina Tabagari, Leqso Nizharadze, Geno Shavdia, Rezo Qatamadze, Givi Intskirveli, Dilar Tchkadua, Lado Jibuti and more. The Big Five are on the verge of fighting crime, investigating situations, facing political antagonists like Ucha Zeragia and Soso Egadze, taking criminal masterminds like Vano Gejadze, Guram Jinoria, Jimsher Philphani, "Kolduna", "Trezora" and others to justice, surviving the corrupted Ministry and the law system in the beginning of the 21st Century.
