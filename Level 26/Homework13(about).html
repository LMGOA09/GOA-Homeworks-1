Our Mission

CityTransit Hub was founded by public transport enthusiasts to document, analyze, and celebrate the systems that keep our cities moving. From municipal bus networks to intricate underground subway systems, we track the fleets and routes that define modern urban mobility.

What We Focus On

  • Fleet Analysis: Inspecting engine specs, passenger capacity, and vehicle designs.
  • Network Mapping: Visualizing how different lines intersect to optimize daily commutes.
  • Community: Building a space where transport fans can share updates and transit photography.
