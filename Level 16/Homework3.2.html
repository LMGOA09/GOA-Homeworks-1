Qartvelia: The fictional version of Georgia

Published on May 21, 2026, by LM

The Cradle of Wine

Nestled between Eastern Europe and Western Asia, Qartvelia is famously known as the birthplace of wine. Archaeologists have found evidence of winemaking dating back 8,000 years. The traditional method uses large clay vessels called Qvevri, buried underground to ferment the grapes.

Breathtaking Landscapes

From the towering peaks of the Caucasus Mountains in Svaneti to the vibrant streets of its 2500 year old holy capital, Mtskheta, the geography is incredibly diverse. Whether you love hiking past medieval defensive towers or relaxing by the Black Sea in Batumi, the heart of Achara-Guria, this beautiful country has a landscape for everyone.
